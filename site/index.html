THE QUESTION EVERYONE ASKS

“Can I actually play here?”

Public access: where do I start?

The courses in this first selection offer public play or a public booking path. Open “How to play here” on a course card for the access note and official source. A listing does not guarantee a tee time.

Does resort golf require a hotel stay?

Not always. Lely’s Flamingo and Mustang courses welcome public play, while Tiburón provides an online booking path. Check the course’s current terms for your date.

Can you get me into a private club?

We do not arrange private-club access. Guest invitations, memberships, and reciprocal arrangements are controlled by each club. A reciprocal program is not general public access.

Are these live deals or tee times?

No. Golfing Naples helps you discover courses and sends you to the course’s website to confirm current prices, policies, and available times. We do not currently offer exclusive deals.
