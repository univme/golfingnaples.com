# Golfing Naples brand direction

Public name: **Golfing Naples**. Domain: **golfingnaples.com**.

Promise: Help residents and visitors find where to play, understand access, and reach the right booking destination.

Homepage line: **Good days start on the green.**

Voice: Warm, knowledgeable, direct, and quietly refined. Use practical explanations, not promises of exclusive access. Avoid unsupported “best,” “unlocked,” and “insider” claims.

Visual identity: Original circular palm-and-flag crest; Golfing / Naples serif wordmark. Emerald #0F3D2E, ivory #F8F7F1, gold #C5A66B, charcoal green #24372D. Libre Caslon Display headings; DM Sans text and controls. Original course illustrations reinforce the field-guide character without misrepresenting venue photography.

Geographic structure: Golfing Naples is the umbrella. Local browsing areas are North Naples, Northeast Naples, and East & South Naples. These are editorial orientation labels. Central Naples and other areas can be introduced when relevant listings are verified. Nearby destinations should retain their own names under a future “Beyond Naples” section.

First conversion: Discover a course and continue to its official booking site.

First revenue opportunity to develop: Verified course offers with an offer owner, eligible audience, terms, expiry, and a reliable checking process. No deals, sponsors, affiliate contracts, or club relationships currently exist in this version.

Next content phase: Course-specific pages with confirmed addresses, booking policies, accessible facilities, licensed photos, and dated editorial notes. Add mailing-list capture only after choosing a delivery provider and contact identity.
